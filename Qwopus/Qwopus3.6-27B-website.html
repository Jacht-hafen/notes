Features

Precision Timing

Accurate to the second. Kessel tracks your water temperature in real-time and starts the steeping countdown the moment it reaches the ideal point.

Smart Brewing Profiles

Curated profiles for green, black, oolong, white, and herbal teas. Each profile sets the optimal temperature and steep time automatically.

Gentle Notifications

Soft chimes replace harsh alarms. Kessel uses calming sound tones that complement the ritual of tea drinking, not interrupt it.
