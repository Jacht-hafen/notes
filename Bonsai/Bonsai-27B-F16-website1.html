Three reasons to love Kessel

Syncs to your phone

Kessel connects via Bluetooth. Get a haptic tap the moment your tea is ready, even if you're on another room.

Presets for every leaf

Green, black, oolong — each tea has its own ideal temperature and steep time. Kessel learns what you like.

Reliable, no battery needed

Powered by a tiny coin cell that lasts four years. The timer keeps running if your phone dies.
